## 🚀 Overview

SwitchCast **v1.4.0** introduces a comprehensive **Background Music Subsystem**, dedicated **Playlists Management**, and direct **SwitchDeck Audio Controls**, transforming SwitchCast into an all-in-one broadcast companion for live presentations and webinars. Presenters can now stream calming background audio or walk-in music independently of live presentation media.

Additionally, v1.4.0 delivers a **Responsive Sources Experience** with queued-only filtering, moves incident diagnostics into Settings, and resolves critical audio concurrency and startup crash conditions.

---

## ✨ What's New & Enhanced in v1.4.0

### 🎵 Background Music Library & Dedicated Playlists
- **Independent Audio Engine (`IBackgroundMusicService`)**: Built a fully decoupled background music player powered by WinRT `Windows.Media.Playback.MediaPlayer`, isolated from video presentation playback.
- **Dedicated Playlists Navigation**: Manage custom music playlists directly from the SwitchCast sidebar. Create, rename, delete playlists, and organize audio files effortlessly.
- **Broad Audio Format Support**: Import and play `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.flac`, `.aac`, `.m4a`, and `.wma` files directly into playlists.
- **Immediate UI Refresh & Sync**: Newly imported audio files instantly appear in playlist views without requiring page reloads or navigation cycling, complemented by a quick-refresh control.
- **Persistent Playlists**: Saved automatically to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SwitchCast\music_playlists.json` with safe atomic writes and corrupt file recovery.

### 🎛️ SwitchDeck Music Transport & Track Picker
- **Presenter Dock Audio Controls**: Control background music playback directly from the lightweight Floating Presenter Dock (SwitchDeck) while presenting full-screen.
- **Live Track Indicator & Marquee**: Displays the currently playing track title, artist metadata, and playback status.
- **Direct Track Selection**: Pick and switch directly to any song in the active playlist via the dock track selector.
- **Dual-Player Audio Isolation**: Hardened audio isolation ensures presentation video audio and background music streams maintain independent volume, muting, and lifecycle states without cross-talk.

### 📋 Responsive Sources & Queue Management
- **Application Windows Default**: The Sources page now defaults to "Application Windows" and intelligently preserves your active category filter across sessions.
- **Queued-Only Filter Toggle**: Filter your sources view to show only assets currently staged in the presentation queue, making dense asset collections easy to manage.
- **Live Queue Summary**: Visual status counter shows total queued items across all categories at a glance.
- **Polite Import Feedback**: Added smooth auto-scroll to newly imported assets and non-blocking in-app notification toasts.

### 🛠️ Diagnostics Architecture Refinement
- **Diagnostics in Settings**: Relocated Incident History and local diagnostics reporting from the Control Dashboard to **Settings > Diagnostics**, keeping the primary presenter dashboard clean and focused.
- **Startup Exception Hardening**: Resolved intermittent WinRT `COMException` (0x80004005) during window initialization and hardened synchronous local crash logging.
